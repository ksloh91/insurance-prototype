Most of us find it easier to talk about holidays, property, or even politics than to ask: *"Mum, what happens if you need long-term care one day?"*

That silence is exactly the **insurance industry taboo** Lumos Advisory was built to break.

## The conversation most families avoid

In Malaysia, adult children often only discover the gaps in their parents' coverage **after** a stroke, fall, or dementia diagnosis. By then:

- Medical cards have stopped paying (hospital discharge = coverage ends)
- Nursing home fees average **RM 7,000/month**
- Siblings argue over who pays — and who cares

Planning early isn't pessimistic. It's an act of love.

## What medical insurance won't cover

Your parents' medical card is important — but it was never designed for:

- Private nurses at home after discharge
- Retirement or nursing home stays
- Home modifications (grab bars, wheelchair ramps)
- Years of ongoing caregiver support

That's where **eldercare and long-term care planning** fills the gap.

## 3 questions to ask your family this weekend

1. **If either parent lost mobility tomorrow, where would they live — and who would pay?**
2. **Do we have RM 7,000/month in spare cash for 4+ years of care?** (the Malaysian average)
3. **Would we rather protect our savings now — or drain our EPF later?**

You don't need all the answers today. You just need to start the conversation.
