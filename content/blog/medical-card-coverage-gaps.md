Medical inflation in Malaysia continues to outpace general inflation. If your medical card was purchased more than five years ago, there's a strong chance your annual limit, room & board cap, or co-payment structure no longer matches actual hospital costs.

## Key areas to review

1. **Annual limit** — Is RM 100,000 still enough for a major surgery plus ICU stay?
2. **Room & board** — Many older plans cap at RM 200/day while private hospitals charge RM 400+.
3. **Co-payment / deductible** — Newer plans may have better cost-sharing structures.
4. **Critical illness rider** — Hospital bills are only part of the story; income replacement matters too.
