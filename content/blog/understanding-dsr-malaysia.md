Debt Service Ratio (DSR) is the method banks use to assess whether you can afford the monthly instalments on a new loan.

## The formula

Your DSR is essentially your total monthly debt commitments divided by your net monthly income. Each bank sets its own maximum DSR threshold — some accept up to 80%, others cap at 50% depending on your profile.

## What counts toward DSR

- Existing housing loan instalments
- Car loans and personal loans
- Credit card minimum payments
- The proposed new loan instalment
